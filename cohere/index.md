## Overview

Cohere+ was a three-year initiative (2022–2025) co-funded by the EU Erasmus+ programme, led by Life Itself in partnership with Emerge, Ekskäret Foundation, the Institute for Integral Studies (Freiburg), and The Hague Center for Global Governance, Innovation, and Emergence.

The project aimed to map the European "transformational change" field, engage with agents to build community and coherence, and develop the collective capacities of change agents working toward regenerative, cohesive societies.
