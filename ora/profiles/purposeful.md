Purposeful is a feminist hub for girls activism, rooted in Africa and working all around the world. We believe that another world is not only possible, it is already being built right here and now, in the ways that girls are organising with each other, imagining with each other, pushing us all a little further towards liberation.,

## Paradigmatic

Their focus on power and girls is a way to get to the root of "the interlocking forces of patriarchy, imperialism and white supremacy" and how these show up in concrete ways in girls' lives, e.g. in issues of health, legislation, and other (social, cultural, and economic) forms of violence and discrimination. 

Their strategy, vision, and theory of change are designed around the goal of world transformation, through their power-building lens and approach of transforming existing power dynamics and structures with and for girls. 

Their whole approach is based on the understanding that liberation (and thus any meaningful kind of long-term transformation) is collective.

## Integrated

Their Theory of Change is informed by Gender@Work, a 4-quadrant framework integrating individual-systemic and formal-informal axes. 

They work with an analysis of individual, collective, communal, and structural change as "overlapping multidirectional and mutually reinforcing".

Their 8 core values include: "Many ways of knowing", "Deep embodiment", "Centering lived expertise", "Cultivating radical imagination", and "Joy as resistance"

Strategic focus on girls' rights and wellbeing, including self-care practices and the necessary integration of the latter in capacity building and leadership development.

## Pragmatic

Their mission (Strategic Framework 2021-2025) is: "Girl activists and their allies have access to the resources,
networks and platforms they need to power their activism and
remake the world for themselves".

One of their core values is "Taking bold action".

## Additional Notes

They have 2 offices: Sierra Leone and London. They have grantees all over the world. See: https://wearepurposeful.org/who-we-are/where-we-work/.
